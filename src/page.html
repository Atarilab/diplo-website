Self-distilled Diffusion Planning for Loco-manipulation

A diffusion planner generates whole-body robot and object motion for a future horizon, and a single RL tracking policy executes it. Two post-training stages ensue: Self-Distillation Diffusion Policy Optimization (SD:DPO) removes the need for strong task conditioning that is required for strong pretraining performance, allowing us to plan for motions using the robot's state history and goal alone, and Diffusion-Augmented PPO (DA:PPO) allows us to finetune the diffusion planner to achieve maximum task success. The resulting planner-tracker pipeline is deployed on the real robot without any real-world finetuning.

Method

From 29 human motions to 7,184 feasible robot motions

Each human motion is retargeted to the robot by inverse kinematics (IK) based methods. This retargeting ignores dynamics and contact forces. Sampling-based trajectory optimization (SBTO) re-solves every clip in simulation from perturbed initial conditions and gives several dynamically feasible solutions for it. Mirroring and quarter turns of the object multiply the set again.

  • 90.0 → 96.5%pretrained survival with one motion per clip vs. SBTO variations and symmetry
  • 72.3 → 96.4%task success after DA:PPO with one motion per clip vs. SBTO variations and symmetry

A diffusion planner and a tracker, trained separately

The planner is a Diffusion Transformer (DiT) model that jointly predicts robot and object motion for a future horizon. It is conditioned on the robot's state history, the object goal and the IK-retargeted human motion. Root and object orientations diffuse on SO(3). A single tracking policy is trained with RL to follow all the feasible motions in our dataset with residual joint targets.

  • 64 framesplanned per chunk, 1.28 s ahead, 10 denoising steps
  • 50 Hztracking policy; joint PD control at 200 Hz
  • SO(3)rotation diffusion raises survival from 85.9% (Euclidean 6D) to 96.5%

SD:DPO distills the prior away

The student is the pretrained planner with its task-conditioning token (the IK-retargeted motion) nulled. It rolls out with the frozen tracker. Every plan it makes is noised to a random denoising step k and denoised twice by the same network: once with the IK-retargeted motion (the self-teacher) and once without (the student). The student's prediction is matched to the teacher's at that same step.

  • no rewardand no critic: the teacher's denoising step is the target
  • on-policystates come from the student's own closed-loop rollouts
  • 57.7 → 100%pick-carry-place survival without the IK-retargeted motion

DA:PPO aligns the plans with the tracker

The distilled planner is fine-tuned with Diffusion-Augmented PPO, a maximum-entropy RL method for diffusion models. The frozen tracker executes each plan in simulation and the rollout reward is credited to every denoising step. A per-step entropy term keeps the plans diverse.

  • 55.1 → 98.8%task success after SD:DPO, then DA:PPO
